Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Clear billing terms

Day Passes and Study Credits are one-time digital purchases. Plus, Pro, and Super are recurring subscriptions when purchased on a monthly or annual basis.

Before you complete a purchase, StudentSpark will display the applicable price, billing interval, and other material billing terms through Stripe Checkout.

By purchasing a recurring subscription, you authorize StudentSpark to charge the payment method provided at the applicable recurring interval until you cancel the subscription.

Cancel a recurring subscription

You may cancel a paid recurring subscription at any time through the Manage or cancel membership button in StudentSpark, which opens Stripe's customer portal.

Unless a different option is clearly shown at the time of cancellation, your subscription will remain active through the end of the billing period that you have already paid for and will not automatically renew for another billing period.

Cancellation does not automatically provide a refund for the unused portion of a billing period unless otherwise required by applicable law or stated by StudentSpark.

A promotional trial that does not automatically convert into a paid subscription does not create a paid subscription that needs to be canceled.

Refund requests

Digital access to StudentSpark is generally provided immediately after purchase. Accordingly, used Day Passes, used Study Credits, and time already used during a subscription are generally not automatically refundable.

However, you may contact StudentSpark if:

  • you were charged more than once for the same purchase;
  • you believe a charge was unauthorized;
  • you paid but did not receive the access or service described at checkout; or
  • you experienced another billing or access problem.

Use the in-app Support form promptly so we can review the transaction and account information. When appropriate, StudentSpark may correct the issue or issue a refund through Stripe.

If StudentSpark terminates a paid subscription without the customer's request, StudentSpark may provide a refund or other appropriate remedy for any prepaid period in which the customer will no longer have access, subject to applicable law.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits or waives any consumer right or remedy that cannot legally be limited or waived under applicable law.

Nothing in this policy prevents you from contacting your bank, card issuer, or payment provider regarding an unauthorized transaction.

Need help?

Use the in-app Support form to contact StudentSpark about billing, cancellation, refunds, or account-access issues. This allows the StudentSpark team to locate the appropriate account and transaction information.
