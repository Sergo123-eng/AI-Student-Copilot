Refunds & Cancellations

Effective September 5, 2026

Canceling a subscription

Recurring Plus, Pro, and Super plans can be canceled through the Stripe customer portal. Unless Stripe presents another option at cancellation, access continues until the end of the already-paid billing period and will not renew.

Day passes and study credits

Day passes and study-credit purchases are one-time digital purchases. Because access is available immediately, they are generally not refundable after use.

When to contact support

If you were charged in error, charged twice, or could not access a paid plan after successful payment, use the in-app Support form promptly. We will review the transaction and, where appropriate, correct the issue or process a refund through Stripe.

Nothing in this notice limits consumer rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.
