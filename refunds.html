Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated September 5, 2026

Clear billing terms

A Day Pass and Study Credits are one-time digital purchases. Plus, Pro, and Super are recurring subscriptions when purchased monthly or annually. The price and renewal interval are shown in Stripe Checkout before payment is submitted.

Cancel a recurring subscription

You may cancel a paid recurring membership through the Manage or cancel membership button in StudentSpark. It opens Stripe’s customer portal. Unless Stripe shows a different choice at the time you cancel, your subscription remains available through the paid billing period and does not renew after that. A promotional trial is not a paid subscription, so there is nothing to cancel.

Refund requests

Digital access is delivered immediately. For that reason, used Day Passes, used Study Credits, and time already used in a subscription are generally not automatically refundable. If you believe you were charged twice, charged without authorization, or paid but did not receive the access shown in Checkout, use the in-app Support form promptly. We will review the request and, when appropriate, correct the issue or issue a refund through Stripe.

Your legal rights

This policy does not limit any consumer right that cannot legally be waived. Nothing here prevents you from contacting your payment provider about an unauthorized charge.

Need help?

Use the in-app Support form so the StudentSpark team can locate the correct account without publishing a personal contact address.
