Privacy Notice

Last updated September 5, 2026

This notice explains how StudentSpark Copilot handles information when a student uses the service. It is written in plain language and is not a substitute for rights that apply under law.

Information we receive

  • Your .edu email address and access or plan status.
  • The questions, schedule details, and study preferences you choose to submit.
  • Messages sent through the in-app Support form.
  • Basic technical and security information needed to prevent abuse and keep the service working.

Why we use it

We use this information to provide study features, authenticate access, process support requests, protect the service, and improve reliable general academic answers. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising profiles.

Shared answer cache

To avoid repeating an AI request, StudentSpark may temporarily store a general, non-personal academic question and a source-aware answer. We exclude personal guidance, schedules, support requests, identifying details, and account information from this shared cache. Cached answers are used only when a later question is sufficiently similar and appropriate.

Payments and service providers

Stripe processes payments. StudentSpark does not receive or store full payment-card numbers. The service may use Vercel for hosting, Supabase for application data, Anthropic for AI responses, and Resend for transactional support email. These providers process data as needed to provide their services. If anonymous web analytics is enabled, it is used to understand site performance, not to sell or target student data.

Retention and choices

We keep data only as long as reasonably needed to operate the service, resolve support issues, comply with obligations, and protect against fraud. You may use the in-app Support form to ask a privacy question or request help with account data. You can cancel recurring paid memberships in the Stripe customer portal.

Please do not submit highly sensitive information

Do not send passwords, payment-card data, government identification numbers, or highly sensitive medical, legal, or financial information in a prompt. StudentSpark is an educational tool, not medical, mental-health, legal, or financial advice.
