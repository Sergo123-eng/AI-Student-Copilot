Privacy Notice

Effective September 5, 2026

StudentSpark Copilot collects the information needed to provide study support: a student email address, plan and access information, support requests, and prompts sent to the service. We use this information to operate the product, prevent abuse, process support requests, and improve reliable general study answers.

How information is handled

Payments are processed by Stripe. StudentSpark does not receive or store full card details. General, non-personal academic questions and source-backed answers may be stored in a shared answer cache for a limited period to reduce repeated AI processing. Personal guidance, schedules, identifying details, and support requests are not placed in that shared cache.

Service providers

StudentSpark uses Vercel for hosting, Supabase for application data, Anthropic for AI responses, Stripe for payments, and Resend for transactional support email. Each provider processes information only as needed to provide its service.

Your choices

You may request account-data assistance or ask a privacy question through the in-app Support form. You can cancel recurring subscriptions through the Stripe customer portal. StudentSpark does not sell personal information.

Important limits

Do not put highly sensitive personal, medical, financial, or account-password information in prompts. StudentSpark is an educational tool, not medical, mental-health, legal, or financial advice.
